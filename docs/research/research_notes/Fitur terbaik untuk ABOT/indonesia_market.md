# Indonesian Gym-Goer and Fitness-App Market (2024–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-10. Method: web search (search-engine summaries of the linked pages). Several hosts could not be fetched in full from this session (e27.co, insight.jakpat.net, health.detik.com, fortuneidn.com were outside the allowed network), so findings from those sources rest on search snippets, not full-page reads. Treat figures attributed to them as snippet-level.

## 1. Gym market size, growth, gym types, membership prices

### Takeaway
Indonesia's gym/club market is small in revenue (roughly USD 600M in 2024, low single-digit CAGR) but its centre of gravity has moved from mall "big box" gyms to cheap shophouse (ruko) chains like Fit Hub (~Rp249k/month, ~100 locations), while premium chains (Gold's Gym) have closed branches amid a price war. Budget gyms run about Rp150k–200k/month, with old-style local gyms far cheaper.

### Cited Findings
- Gym, health and fitness clubs in Indonesia generated ~USD 600M (~Rp10 trillion) revenue in 2024, a 4.4% CAGR for 2019–2024 (vendor estimate) — [Ezypay guide to Indonesia](https://www.ezypay.com/blog/ezypays-guide-to-indonesia)
- Ezypay also states ~67% of Indonesian gym-goers are aged 18–24 (vendor estimate; methodology not visible) — [Ezypay](https://www.ezypay.com/blog/ezypays-guide-to-indonesia)
- A Jakpat gym-habit report (~2,000 respondents) estimated the gym industry at Rp2–3 trillion per year, which conflicts sharply with Ezypay's ~Rp10 trillion; scope/definition differences likely — [Jakpat: The Habit of Going to the Gym](https://insight.jakpat.net/the-habit-of-going-to-the-gym-jakpat-survey-report/) (snippet only); contradicted by [Ezypay](https://www.ezypay.com/blog/ezypays-guide-to-indonesia)
- Broader "sportstech and digital fitness" market growth cited at 15.7% in 2025 (paid research summary; much wider scope than gyms) — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/indonesia-sportstech-and-digital-fitness-market)
- Sports equipment and fitness retail ~USD 2.5B in 2025, 7.6% forecast CAGR 2026–2032 (paid research summary) — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/indonesia-sports-equipment-and-fitness-retail-market.md)
- Trend: pivot from big-box mall gyms to "agile shophouse startups"; FitHub reportedly in 25+ cities with ~100 locations (July 2025) — [e27](https://e27.co/indonesias-fitness-pivot-from-big-box-gyms-at-the-mall-to-agile-shophouse-startups-20250727/) (snippet only)
- Fit Hub official site: "Affordable Gym ... Start from Rp 249.000", access to all Fit Hub gyms plus free daily classes (undated page) — [fithub.id](https://fithub.id/en)
- Earlier Fit Hub price: from Rp239,000/month (SWA, ~2023), strategy built on location and affordable price — [SWA](https://swa.co.id/read/403056/lokasi-hingga-harga-terjangkau-jadi-taktik-fit-hub-jaring-pelanggan)
- Fitness First Indonesia (sister brand of Celebrity Fitness) Home Club plan from Rp640,000/month — [Fitness First ID](https://www.fitnessfirst.com/id/en/membership)
- Celebrity Fitness Indonesia publishes no list price; club rules say monthly fees rise every 12 months and late payment incurs a Rp50,000/month fee — [Celebrity Fitness club rules](https://www.celebrityfitness.com/id/id/club-rules)
- Gold's Gym closed branches in 2025 despite high interest in exercise; a 26-year-old Jakarta member said the price gap between premium and ruko gyms is significant ("millions") — [detikHealth, Gold's Gym Tutup](https://health.detik.com/kebugaran/d-8002209/golds-gym-tutup-di-tengah-tingginya-minat-berolahraga-ada-apa) (snippet only)
- 2025 "price war": gyms closing, mid/lower segments squeezed because consumers compare prices and switch to cheaper promos — [Fortune IDN](https://www.fortuneidn.com/luxury/gym-berguguran-di-tengah-perang-harga-ini-masalahnya-00-4vfn9-jp9mq7) (snippet only)
- Jakarta budget gyms listed under Rp500k: one at Rp175k/month, Gorilla Gym at Rp200k/month (undated roundup) — [Popmama](https://www.popmama.com/life/health/rekomendasi-tempat-gym-dengan-biaya-keanggotaan-di-bawah-500-ribu-00-vbjw7-sprhgr)
- Surabaya cheap gyms (2023): entries from Rp30,000–Rp40,000 (likely per visit or very low monthly; article from 2023) — [detikJatim](https://www.detik.com/jatim/berita/d-6928587/5-tempat-gym-murah-di-surabaya)
- Populix (Nov 2024, n=1,030): running is the most common exercise, then cycling, swimming, then gym (26%) — via [Media Asuransi News](https://mediaasuransinews.co.id/ekonomi-bisnis/survei-populix-94-orang-indonesia-rutin-berolahraga/) (search snippet)
- Lack of time is a leading reason people find it hard to exercise — [Databoks/Katadata](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/consumer-services/statistics/67bbd1c5e6d84/survey-many-people-find-it-difficult-to-exercise-due-to-lack-of-time)

### Inferences
- Core ABOT user is likely 18–30, on a budget ruko gym (Fit Hub tier, Rp150k–250k/month) or a cheap local gym. A PRO price should feel small next to that membership (e.g. well under 1/5 of a monthly gym fee).
- Gym switching driven by promos means users may change gyms often: equipment-agnostic routines and easy exercise swaps (machine brand varies, plate-loaded vs pin-stack) matter more than gym-specific setup.
- Cheap local gyms (gym rakyat) likely have older free weights, few cable/specialty machines; a strong barbell/dumbbell library and substitutions will fit better than a machine-heavy catalogue (inference, not sourced).

### Gaps
- No primary 2025–2026 figure for number of gyms or gym members in Indonesia. Statista/Euromonitor figures paywalled.
- No current Celebrity Fitness Indonesia or Gold's Gym Indonesia price list found.
- No source quantified "gym rakyat" (neighbourhood gym) prices for 2025; equipment availability not documented by any source found.
- Jakpat gym-habit report details (frequency, goals, spending) could not be fetched.

## 2. Fitness app usage, Android vs iOS, device tiers, data costs

### Takeaway
Indonesia is overwhelmingly Android (~78% of mobile web traffic, mid-2026) and dominated by price-sensitive brands (Xiaomi, Transsion/Infinix/TECNO) selling sub-Rp3M phones; mobile data is cheap (~USD 0.28/GB in 2023). No survey names which gym-logging apps Indonesians use; Hevy and Strong appear in Indonesian recommendations, and an Indonesian-language competitor (Fitwill) exists.

### Inferences
- Android-first is correct; design for 3–4 GB RAM Infinix/Redmi-class phones, older Android versions, aggressive OEM battery killers (Xiaomi MIUI/HyperOS, Transsion XOS) that can kill rest-timer notifications (inference; consistent with device mix).
- Data is cheap and speeds decent, so data cost is not the main constraint; storage and RAM on low-end phones and battery are. A small APK and offline-first behaviour matter more than bandwidth savings.
- With ~22% iOS, the iOS/web user is a minority but likely higher-spend (inference).
- Hevy's generous free tier sets the expectation that logging itself is free; PRO must sell extras.

### Gaps
- No download/MAU data for Hevy, Strong, Fitbod, Fitwill in Indonesia (Sensor Tower/data.ai paywalled; Similarweb blocked).
- No found Indonesian-made gym logging app with notable traction; local apps found are mostly home-workout or general wellness.
- No 2025 cable.co.uk per-GB figure for Indonesia.
- No data on typical RAM/storage of installed base.

Consumers are used to monthly subscriptions in the Rp30k–70k range (Spotify, YouTube), with student tiers around Rp30k–40k. Evidence on willingness to pay specifically for fitness apps is thin.

### Inferences
- QRIS should be the default/first checkout option; DANA, GoPay and ShopeePay all need to be present (no single leader); VA is a secondary fallback for bank-transfer users.
- Price anchors: Spotify Student ~Rp30k and Individual ~Rp60k/month. A gym-tracker PRO around Rp15k–35k/month, or a yearly plan near Rp100k–200k, would sit below those anchors (estimate). A lifetime/one-time option suits the meaningful "pay once" segment (MEF finding, older).
- Because QRIS/e-wallet payments are push payments (no card-on-file auto-renew), subscription renewals through Midtrans likely require manual re-pay; prepaid periods (1/3/12 months) and a one-time lifetime tier fit this better than auto-renewing subscriptions (inference from payment-method mechanics).
- Subscription fatigue (Bango) argues for clear expiry reminders and no surprise renewals.

### Gaps
- No Midtrans or Xendit primary report with 2025 Indonesian payment-method mix found.
- No data on what Indonesians pay for fitness apps specifically, or on Hevy/Strong local IDR pricing.
- Statista subscription survey results paywalled.

## 4. Cultural and seasonal factors: Ramadan, goals, creators, local food

### Takeaway
Ramadan reshapes training for roughly a month: Indonesian health media recommend lifting either shortly before iftar or after iftar, at reduced volume (30–60 min, fewer sets), prioritising weights over hard cardio. TikTok is the dominant daily-time platform, and the fitness creator scene is fragmented. Local protein staples (tempe ~20.8 g/100 g, tahu ~10.9 g/100 g per TKPI) need first-class support in any nutrition feature.

### Cited Findings
- Studies cited by Hellosehat suggest ~14:00–16:00, before iftar, as an ideal Ramadan training window so recovery starts at iftar; beginners ~30 min with light loads — [Hellosehat, Panduan fitness saat puasa](https://hellosehat.com/kebugaran/olahraga-lainnya/panduan-fitness-saat-puasa/)
- IDN Times: best time depends on goal; weight training generally better after iftar when energy is restored; 30–60 min sessions sufficient — [IDN Times, Jadwal gym selama puasa](https://www.idntimes.com/health/fitness/jadwal-gym-selama-puasa-ramadan-q9t01-00-s66xw-1hfz85)
- Reduce intensity and volume (fewer sets/reps) but keep moderate lifting to limit muscle loss — [IDN Times, penyesuaian latihan](https://www.idntimes.com/health/fitness/rekomendasi-penyesuaian-latihan-di-gym-saat-puasa-ramadan-00-92d83-rbqgrm/amp)
- Doctor (detikHealth): weight training is lower dehydration risk than cardio when done at relaxed tempo — [detikHealth](https://health.detik.com/kebugaran/d-8380870/kapan-waktu-terbaik-ngegym-di-bulan-puasa-ini-penjelasan-dokter)
- Avoid high-intensity cardio during fasting; prioritise weights — [Fortune IDN](https://www.fortuneidn.com/news/tips-olahraga-pada-saat-puasa-ramadan-00-xkln4-p9m71b/amp)
- Indonesian brand content also covers "gym saat puasa" timing, duration and exercise choice — [SVRG](https://svrg.id/en/blogs/the-journey/gym-saat-puasa)
- TikTok ~1h53m/day per user, effectively tied with WhatsApp — [We Are Social](https://wearesocial.com/id/blog/2025/11/digital-2026-top-digital-and-social-media-trends-in-indonesia/)
- Indonesian fitness creators listed by vendor rankings include Brian Yeremia (~91.5k TikTok followers, gym and diet tips), Sally Tanudjaja (running/fitness), Bodyfitbybagus and Flow with Mira (YouTube), Dinda Annisa (female fitness TikTok); rankings are vendor-algorithmic — [Heepsy TikTok gym](https://www.heepsy.com/top-tiktok/gym/indonesia); [Favikon](https://www.favikon.com/blog/top-fitness-influencers-indonesia); [Heepsy YouTube](https://heepsy.com/top-youtube/fitness/indonesia); [Heepsy female fitness](https://www.heepsy.com/top-tiktok/female-fitness/indonesia)
- Jakpat 2024: 75% of people are happy to use local sports products — [SWA](https://swa.co.id/read/448381/jakpat-75-orang-senang-pakai-produk-olah-raga-lokal)
- Protein per 100 g (TKPI): tempe ~20.8 g, tahu ~10.9 g; other sources give tahu 8–9.4 g — [detikHealth, Tempe vs Tahu](https://health.detik.com/diet/d-8440165/tempe-vs-tahu-mana-yang-lebih-unggul-nutrisinya); [CNBC Indonesia](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/lifestyle/20250825105531-33-661019/tahu-vs-tempe-mana-yang-lebih-kaya-protein)
- Chicken breast ~23 g raw / ~30–31 g cooked per 100 g; one large egg ~6 g — [Halodoc](https://www.halodoc.com/artikel/100-gr-dada-ayam-berapa-protein-ini-jawabannya)

### Inferences
- A "Ramadan mode" is a concrete local differentiator: shift reminders to pre-iftar or post-tarawih windows, suggest reduced volume (e.g. drop a set, cap session ~45 min), pause or soften streak/overload pressure, and add sahur/iftar hydration and protein pacing. Dates move ~11 days earlier each year (Ramadan 2027 roughly Feb 2027), so this should be date-driven and user-toggleable (inference).
- Share cards sized for TikTok/IG Stories, with Indonesian captions, fit the platform mix.
- Nutrition presets should use Indonesian portions (potong tempe, butir telur, centong nasi) rather than grams only (inference).

### Gaps
- No survey quantifying how many Indonesian lifters keep training during Ramadan or how much they cut volume.
- No data on popularity of "cutting/bulking/body goals" as stated goals; terms are common in content but unquantified.
- No reliable, dated ranking of top Indonesian gym creators by reach.

## 5. Language: exercise naming and units

### Takeaway
Indonesian gym talk keeps English exercise and programming terms (bench press, squat, deadlift, set, rep, PR, 1RM, bulking, cutting, pump, spotting) inside Indonesian sentences; kilograms are standard. No source documents a formal Indonesian exercise vocabulary, which suggests keeping English exercise names with Indonesian UI copy.

### Cited Findings
- Indonesian gym glossary (IDN Times) explains RM (1RM, 2RM, 3RM) and "spotting" using the English terms within Indonesian text — [IDN Times, Istilah dalam gym](https://www.idntimes.com/sport/arena/istilah-dalam-gym-00-2hm81-m6rvrc)
- Indonesian health/fitness articles use "ngegym", "gym saat puasa", "latihan beban" alongside English terms — [detikHealth](https://health.detik.com/kebugaran/d-8380870/kapan-waktu-terbaik-ngegym-di-bulan-puasa-ini-penjelasan-dokter); [SVRG](https://svrg.id/en/blogs/the-journey/gym-saat-puasa)
- Indonesian gym-price articles quote kg-based equipment and IDR pricing; Indonesia uses the metric system (general knowledge, not separately sourced)

### Inferences
- Keep exercise names in English (searchable by English name) with Indonesian aliases for common ones (e.g. "angkat beban", "dada", "punggung", "kaki" as muscle-group labels) so search works both ways.
- Use kg by default for id-ID; lb only as opt-in.
- Verbs like "ngegym", "latihan", "istirahat" suit casual Indonesian copy; formal "Anda" may feel stiff for an 18–30 audience (inference; register not surveyed).

### Gaps
- No corpus or survey of Indonesian lifter vocabulary; slang like "anak gym" / "sobat gym" not documented in sources found.

## 6. Local competitors and gaps

### Takeaway
No Indonesian-built strength-logging app with clear traction surfaced. Competition is global trackers (Hevy, Strong) without local payments, plus Indonesian-language generalist apps (Fitwill) and gym-chain apps (Fit Hub booking). The open space: bilingual, Android-light, offline, paid in QRIS/e-wallet at local prices, with Ramadan and local-food awareness.

### Cited Findings
- Hevy and Strong are the reference trackers; Hevy is free-tier-generous with community features, Strong is older and seen as stagnant by some reviewers — [Garage Gym Reviews](https://garagegymreviews.com/?p=211181); [Similarweb](https://similarweb.com/app/google/io.strongapp.strong/vs/com.hevy)
- Fitwill offers an Indonesian-language log/planner — [fitwill.app/id](https://fitwill.app/id)
- Fit Hub's own offering bundles daily classes and multi-club access, and runs a personal-trainer service — [fithub.id](https://fithub.id/en); [Fit Hub trainers](https://fithub.id/en/trainer)
- Vertex Ventures sees health and fitness (padel to Pilates) as Indonesia's next consumer wave (2025) — [Vertex Ventures](https://www.vertexventures.sg/news/from-padel-to-pilates-health-and-fitness-are-powering-indonesia-s-next-consumer-wave/)

### Inferences
- Global apps bill via Google Play in IDR; ABOT's direct QRIS/e-wallet checkout is a differentiator for users without cards or Play balance (inference; Google Play carrier billing/e-wallet top-up options exist but weren't researched here).
- Fast logging that works with no signal (basement gyms in ruko/mall buildings often have poor reception, unverified) is a practical edge.

### Gaps
- Play Store listings for Indonesian fitness apps could not be checked in this session; no install counts gathered.
- Nearby SEA (Malaysia, Philippines, Vietnam) markets not covered beyond incidental data (e.g. Fitness First Malaysia Tri-Access RM389/month — [Fitness First MY](https://www.fitnessfirst.com/my/en/membership/Tri-Access-Membership)).
