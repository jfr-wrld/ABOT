# Pricing and Monetization Benchmarks for Gym Tracker Apps (Indonesia focus, 2025-2026)

Method note: WebFetch could not reach revenuecat.com or sensai.fit from this session (DNS blocked by the session network allowlist), so every figure below comes from search-result snippets of the cited pages, not a full read of the page. RevenueCat figures in particular are often quoted second-hand; where two numbers conflict, both are listed. IDR conversions marked "(converted)" use an assumed rate of about Rp16,500 per USD. That rate is not taken from a cited source and should be checked before use.

## 1. What do comparable gym/workout apps charge, and what do they gate?

### Takeaway
Pure loggers (Hevy, Strong, Liftosaur, Setgraph) cluster at USD 3-5 a month, USD 24-40 a year and USD 75-200 lifetime, with a usable free tier capped on routines or history. "Smart coach" apps (Fitbod, Alpha Progression, JEFIT Elite, MacroFactor, Boostcamp Pro) charge USD 60-96 a year. I found no confirmed Google Play Indonesia IDR price for any of them.

  - lifetime ≈ Rp866,000.

## 3. Monetization data (RevenueCat, Adapty, regional)

### Takeaway
Health & Fitness is the best-converting subscription category:
- trial-to-paid median about 40% (2025);
- hard paywalls convert downloads to paid about 5x better than freemium (around 11-12% vs around 2% at day 35);
- annual plans drive about 59-68% of category revenue.

Indonesia is a lower-price market (Adapty index 0.7). Indonesia-specific conversion data is essentially unavailable publicly.

### Cited Findings
**Trial-to-paid**
- Health & Fitness median trial-to-paid is 39.9%, top decile 68.3% (RevenueCat 2025) — [Athletech News](https://athletechnews.com/fitness-apps-monetizable-winner-take-all-or-most/); [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)
- The 2026 edition says Health & Fitness leads on median trial-to-paid. Snippets cite "top performers over 23%", likely a different metric (for example download-to-paid), so the two editions are not directly comparable — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)

**Hard paywall vs freemium (download-to-paid, day 35)**
- 2026 page: hard paywall 10.7% vs freemium 2.1% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- 2025 report: 12.1% vs 2.2% — [Airbridge](https://www.airbridge.io/en/blog/hard-paywall-vs-freemium-2026); [ARPU Brothers](https://arpubrothers.com/blog/2025-saas-mobile-apps-trends/)
- Year-one retention is about equal: 27% for hard paywall vs 28% for freemium (2026) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- Refund rates are higher for hard paywalls: 5.8% vs 3.4% for freemium (2025) — [ARPU Brothers](https://arpubrothers.com/blog/2025-saas-mobile-apps-trends/)

**Annual vs monthly**
- Health & Fitness annual revenue share: 59% (RevenueCat 2026) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- Other figures:
  - 60.6% — [Medium summary](https://chuvak-pavel.medium.com/state-of-app-monetization-2026-key-trends-from-revenuecat-and-adapty-8bdc23a4bb6f);
  - 67% of plans annual — [Airbridge category benchmarks](https://www.airbridge.io/en/blog/subscription-app-pricing-by-category-2026-benchmark)
- Health & Fitness apps are the most likely to use a mixed trial strategy, at 56% (2025). The category also leads in payer LTV — [Athletech News](https://athletechnews.com/fitness-apps-monetizable-winner-take-all-or-most/)

**Regional (Adapty)**
- Indonesia has a pricing index of 0.7 against a US baseline of 1.0, in the lower tier with Turkey and India. Prices vary up to 4x between countries — [Adapty State of In-App Subscriptions 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions/)
- Apple Ads: install-to-paid converts about 4x better in Indonesia than in the US, per a snippet whose attribution is ambiguous — [Adapty Apple Ads benchmarks](https://adapty.io/apple-ads-for-subscription-apps/)
- Global averages (Adapty 2026): install-to-trial 10.9%, trial-to-paid 25.6%. Weekly plans convert 1.7-7.4x better than annual — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-report/); [RocketShip HQ summary](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/adapty-subscription-app-benchmark-2025-summary/)
- Adapty 2025 edition: US trial-to-paid 28%; install-to-trial under 12% in most regions — [Adapty 2025 in 10 minutes](https://adapty.io/blog/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-2025-in-10-minutes/)

### Inferences
- For an offline logger whose core value is the free log, a full hard paywall conflicts with ABOT's "free tier keeps core logging" plan and with competitor norms. A hybrid fits better:
  - a freemium core;
  - a contextual paywall at PRO features;
  - an onboarding paywall with a trial.

  The hard-paywall uplift data argues for showing the paywall prominently during onboarding, even when it can be dismissed.
- Expect annual to be the revenue driver (around 60%). The paywall should default-select annual.
- Indonesia's 0.7 index supports pricing below US list, but ABOT's annual price (about 0.5x Hevy US) is already below that index. There may be room to test Rp249,000-279,000 annual later.

### Gaps
- No public Indonesia or SEA breakdown of trial-to-paid, download-to-paid or plan mix from RevenueCat or Adapty. These sit behind gated report pages.
- No Sensor Tower or data.ai SEA health-and-fitness revenue data found within the call budget.
- RevenueCat primary pages could not be fetched. Figures should be verified against the PDFs.

## 4. Lifetime licences and founder offers: cannibalization and capping

### Takeaway
Lifetime plans are uncommon (5-18% of paywalls). The main risk is that they capture exactly the loyal users who would have renewed. The common guidance is to price lifetime at about 3-4x annual or higher. I found no hard data on time-limited founder offers.

### Cited Findings
- Lifetime options appear on 5-18% of paywalls depending on category, highest in Gaming at 18% (RevenueCat 2026, cited second-hand) — [PricePush glossary](https://pricepush.app/glossary/lifetime-purchase)
- Main risk: "your most loyal users, the ones most likely to buy a lifetime deal, are also the ones most likely to keep renewing without one". The source advises checking LTV by cohort before adding one — [Airbridge: should you offer lifetime](https://www.airbridge.io/en/blog/should-you-offer-a-lifetime-subscription)
- Pricing guidance:
  - a range of 2x-12x annual, with successful premium offers at 5x or more — [PricePush](https://pricepush.app/glossary/lifetime-purchase);
  - 3-4x annual as an anchor that makes the subscription look cheap — [AppOpportunity](https://appopportunity.com/blog/how-to-price-indie-app)
- Argument against lifetime for long-term growth — [CMO2CRO Substack](https://cmo2cro.substack.com/p/why-lifetime-pricing-is-a-losing)
- Hevy lifetime purchases persist on the account even when a user joins a Hevy Coach, while monthly and annual subscriptions keep billing. In other words, lifetime is treated as a first-class entitlement — [Hevy pricing via search summary](https://hevy.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- ABOT's Rp798,000 lifetime (4.0x annual) is within the 3-4x guidance.
- The founder price (Rp399,000, 2.0x annual) is at the bottom of the observed range. It should be capped by count, a date window, or both, so it does not become the default purchase. Typical indie practice is a fixed number of seats or a launch window. This is an inference: I found no dataset quantifying it.
- Lifetime revenue is front-loaded and helps early cash flow, but it reduces recurring revenue later. Tracking the share of buyers choosing lifetime vs annual is the key guardrail.

### Gaps
- No quantitative study of founder or early-adopter offer cannibalization was found.
- No data on how many indie fitness apps cap lifetime offers.

## 5. Free trial norms and intro offers

### Takeaway
Longer trials convert better per trial (17-32 days ≈ 42.5% vs about 25.5% for trials of 4 days or less). 3-day and 7-day trials remain the most common. Many 3-day trials are cancelled on Day 0. For a habit app like a logger, 7-14 days fits the "log a few sessions" value moment.

### Cited Findings
- 17-32 day trials: median 42.5% trial-to-paid vs 25.5% for trials under 4 days (RevenueCat 2026). This is about 70% better — [RevenueCat blog: 7-day trial](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/7-day-trial-subscription-app); [RevenueCat 2026 in 10 minutes](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026)
- A conflicting 2025 figure gives 45.7% for 17-32 day trials — [Athletech News](https://athletechnews.com/fitness-apps-monetizable-winner-take-all-or-most/)
- Day-0 cancellations:
  - 3-day trials: 55.4% of cancellations happen on Day 0, and 84% by Day 1;
  - 7-day trials: 39.8% on Day 0 — [RevenueCat blog](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/7-day-trial-subscription-app)
- RevenueCat's own 7-day vs 14-day test: 14 days got slightly more starts but fewer conversions, so RevenueCat reverted to 7 days — [RevenueCat blog](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/7-day-trial-subscription-app)
- Most common trial lengths: 3 days (32% of apps) and 7 days (31%) (Statista via Phiture) — [Phiture](https://phiture.com/mobilegrowthstack/the-subscription-stack-how-to-optimize-trial-length/)
- Competitor practice:
  - Liftosaur offers a 14-day trial — [SensAI](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/liftosaur-review);
  - Apple Fitness+ offers a 1-month trial in Indonesia — [detikInet](https://inet.detik.com/mobile-apps/d-8256746/apa-itu-apple-fitness-dan-harga-langganan-di-indonesia)

### Inferences
- A 7-day trial on annual is the safe default for ABOT. A 14-day trial is worth A/B testing because users need 3-4 logged sessions to see overload and recovery insights.
- Avoid 3-day trials: the Day-0 cancellation pattern is worst there.
- Low-price intro offers (for example the first month at Rp9,900) are plausible for an emerging market, but I found no emerging-market data on intro offers.

### Gaps
- No Indonesia- or emerging-market-specific trial-length or intro-offer data.
- Whether Play Store trials require a payment method that many Indonesian users lack (for example prepaid carrier billing) was not confirmed.

## 6. Payment-method behaviour in Indonesia

### Takeaway
Indonesia is wallet- and QRIS-first, and credit cards are marginal (5% of e-commerce value). Google Play Indonesia accepts DANA, GoPay, OVO and carrier billing (Telkomsel, Indosat, XL/AXIS, Tri, Smartfren). Google notes, however, that some carriers cannot be used for subscriptions and that prepaid numbers may not be supported. That matters directly for recurring-plan conversion.

### Cited Findings
- **Google Play Indonesia payment options:**
  - e-wallets DANA, GoPay and OVO (registered account with balance);
  - carrier billing through Indosat, Smartfren, Telkomsel, XL/AXIS and Tri;
  - carrier billing works only in the Play Store app;
  - prepaid numbers may not be supported;
  - some carriers cannot be used for subscriptions — [Google Play Help (ID)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2651410?hl=en&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DID)
- DANA, GoPay, OVO and mobile billing are subject to Play purchase verification — [Google Play Help: purchase verification](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/1626831?hl=en&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DID)
- Google Play gift cards and vouchers are sold at Alfamart and Indomaret and through OVO/GoPay — [Digicodes](https://digicodes.net/cara-beli-voucher-google-play-gift-card-di-alfamart-indomaret-atau-ovo/)

  - account-to-account 34%;
  - cash 13%;
  - credit cards 5%;
  - BNPL 3%;
  - other 3%.

- Many Indonesian users can pay for one-time purchases, through vouchers, prepaid pulsa or a wallet top-up, more easily than they can sustain auto-renewing subscriptions. This favours offering lifetime and annual (paid once a year) prominently, alongside monthly.
- Because ABOT is offline-first with "no runtime network calls", Google Play Billing is the realistic channel. Direct QRIS checkout would need a server and conflicts with ABOT's no-network constraint. (This is an inference from the project brief in CLAUDE.md.)

## 7. Suggested tier structure and gating best practice for a logger

### Takeaway
Market leaders keep unlimited logging, the exercise library, basic history and data export or retention free. They gate either *volume* (routine count, history depth) or *smart features*. Strong (3 routines) and Hevy (4 routines, 3 months of history) show that routine caps are accepted, but history caps and data lock-in draw criticism.

### Cited Findings
- **Strong** caps the free tier at 3 routines but keeps unlimited workouts. Data stays available whether the user is PRO or not — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id464254577); [AI Tools Bakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/blog/strong-app-review/)
- **Hevy** free tier:
  - 4 routines, 3 months of history and 7 custom exercises;
  - logging, social, Strava and watch apps are free;
  - progressive-overload "Trainer" is Pro;
